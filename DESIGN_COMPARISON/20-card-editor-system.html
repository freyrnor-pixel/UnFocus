UnFocus · card system · editing

One composer, three depths

Adding and editing use the same object at three depths: a quick row inside the card for one field, a sheet over the card for the rest, and a full editor when the thing being made has a shape of its own. Editing an existing item opens the same depth it was created at, prefilled, with Delete in the footer. Nothing is ever saved by leaving — every depth commits with one button, and cancel means cancel.

The three depths, live

Pick a card and a mode. Type in the field to see the save button arm; press More in the quick row to escalate into the sheet without losing what you typed.

What each card asks for

The quick field is the one thing the item cannot exist without. Everything else waits in the sheet, in this order, and every card puts its optional fields last so the sheet can be dismissed early.

Rules

Depth is chosen by the item, not the user

A task, a shopping item and a scanned line open at quick depth. A habit, a health entry and a goal open the sheet. A plan opens the full editor because it has a time, a place and people. The user can always escalate up; they are never dropped a level down.

Where the composer appears

  • Quick: appended inside the card body, under the last row
  • Sheet: over the card, bottom-anchored, card still visible above
  • Full: fills the frame, same geometry as a fullscreen card

Editing reuses creating

Tap a row to edit it. The same composer opens at the same depth, prefilled, the header reads Edit instead of New, the primary button reads Save, and a Delete button joins the footer. No separate edit screen exists.

Commit and cancel

  • Save is disabled until the required field has content
  • Enter in the quick row saves and keeps the row open for the next item
  • Cancel or scrim-tap discards; if fields changed, one confirm
  • No autosave, no silent drafts

Glow budget holds

The focused field takes a 3px feature-tinted ring, not a glow. Only the primary button and the active chip bloom, at the same 16–22px as a card's icon. Inputs, labels and helper text stay unlit.

Motion

Quick row: height and opacity, 220ms. Sheet: translateY from 24px with the scrim fading in, 300ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Escalation quick → sheet keeps the typed value and cross-fades in 200ms. Reduced motion: no translate, opacity only.

Errors are quiet

Validation runs on save, never while typing. The field border turns to the error hue, one line of text under it says what to fix, and the rest of the sheet holds still. Nothing shakes.

Keyboard and reach

The sheet lifts above the keyboard and keeps the save bar pinned to the top of it. The primary field autofocuses at every depth. Chip rows scroll horizontally rather than wrapping past two lines.
