UnFocus · card system

Glass cards, three sizes, one control

Every card on every screen — tasks, habits, shopping, health, plans, scan, hints — is the same object at three sizes: peek, open, and fullscreen. One control cluster sits in the top-right of every card, in the same place, with the same two icons. Glow is spent only where it is functional: the feature icon, an active check, a filled bar.

Two directions

Same structure, same controls, different surface. A keeps the glass neutral and lets the feature colour glow from the icon only. B tints the whole pane in the feature hue and blooms it outward, so a card is identifiable before you read it. Click the controls on either card — they work.

State matrix

Every card, every state. Switch direction and backdrop to compare.

Related cards

Three groups — Growth (Habits · Goals · Health), Supplies (Shopping · Scan), Time (Tasks · Plans). Grouped cards sit adjacent in the stack, and in fullscreen they carry a tab strip so you can move between related cards without going back out. Goals is the new card: a habit with a horizon.

Fullscreen with group tabs
Habits, Goals and Health are one place once you are inside any of them.
Manage cards — per screen
Long-press any card, or Settings → Manage cards. Show/hide, set the size a card opens at, drag to reorder. Group tags show what will travel together.

How the control works

Two icons, never more

Chevron toggles peek ↔ open; the chevron rotates to point the way it will move. Expand-arrows go fullscreen and become collapse-arrows there. Nothing else ever appears in the cluster.

Fullscreen grows in place

The card scales from its own rectangle to fill the screen — no new screen, no separate header. The title stays where the eye left it, the cluster stays in the top-right, so the way back is the button you just pressed.

Reordering hides the cluster

Long-press any card to enter reorder: cards drop to peek height, a grip replaces the icon's left margin, the cluster dims out of reach, and the gap you're dragging into shows as a dashed slot.

Per-card control

The cluster only ever resizes. Everything else a card can do lives in one place: its footer action when small, and the group tab strip plus footer when fullscreen. No overflow menus, no long-press menus per card.

Per-screen control

Manage cards is the only place a screen's composition changes: visibility, opening size, order. It is reachable two ways — long-press a card, or Settings → Manage cards — and it names the group each card belongs to so reordering never separates a pair.

Glow budget

  • Feature icon: rim + 18px + 40px bloom
  • Completed check: 14px bloom
  • Filled bar / ring: 12–16px bloom
  • Text, borders, backgrounds: no glow

Empty stays neutral

Empty states use the shame-free neutral for the dashed well and muted text — the feature colour survives only in the icon, so nothing reads as a warning.

Motion

Height and scale on 340ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), opacity on 240ms, no overshoot. Under reduced motion the card snaps between states and the glow holds still.
