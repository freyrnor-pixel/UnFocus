UnFocus · card system · screen corrections

The five tabs, corrected

Same five screens Code built, rebuilt against the card system and the composer spec. Norwegian copy is kept as it stands in the app. Each screen lists what changed, so the diff can be applied without re-reading the whole handoff.

Screens

352 × 716 frame, dark theme. Every card carries the same anatomy: accent icon, title, peek line, two-icon control cluster, then body. The topbar and the tab bar are the only chrome; nothing else floats.

What was wrong, globally

Glass had nothing to blur

Cards sat on flat near-black, so blur produced uniform grey slabs. The backdrop now carries three soft accent washes per tab; the same card material reads as glass because there is light behind it.

Control cluster drifted

Headers carried three to five controls — bell, mic, camera, lock, ⋮, expand, chevron — in different orders. It is exactly two, always chevron then expand, always the same 29px. Everything else moves into the body or the Manage cards sheet.

Accents are tokens, not hexes

The vivid yellow / green / cyan / pink in the build are literals. Every accent here resolves from var(--feature-*) on the dark scheme — task for Hjem and Gjøremål, shop for Handle, habits for Vaner, health for Helse, capture for Notater, meals for Mat, focus for Mål. If the app is meant to keep those vivid per-tab hues, they belong in tokens/themes.css as a new named scheme, never inline, or scheme switching breaks across 5 schemes × light/dark.

Glow was spent on chrome

Blue halos bloomed behind neutral icon buttons and around the nav. Glow belongs to accent icons, the active chip and the primary button only, and is always the card's own feature hue — never blue on a pink or cyan screen.

Cards were mostly hint text

An italic quote took two thirds of the card and pushed content below the fold. The hint is one slot, two lines maximum, muted, directly under the header, and it never appears on a card that has no content yet — the empty state speaks instead.

Counts read as failure

Collapsed cards showed a bare 0 next to the title. They now show a peek line in words, so a card with nothing in it says what it is for rather than scoring the user zero.

The tab bar clipped the stack

The last card was cut in half behind the bar. The stack scrolls with 96px bottom padding; the bar is inset 14px and blurs what passes under it.

Stray artefacts

Loose 2px dots, a hard black rule inside I dag, and hairline dividers under every header. All removed — separation comes from the body's 11px offset, not a line.

Composer was inconsistent

Five different add affordances appeared across five screens. One quick row: placeholder, context chip, More, Save. Save stays dim until the field has content. Escalation and full editors follow the composer file.
